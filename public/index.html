    Your PC is locked
    
    
    
      
    
        
    The PC is locked, but this needs the PC to be accessible (unlocked). Continue?
    
        
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Unlock your PC the way you normally sign in:
    
        
    • PIN or password typed on the PC
    • Fingerprint or face (Windows Hello) on the PC
    • Security key plugged into the PC
    • From here, with Chrome Remote Desktop: you see the PC's lock screen and type your PIN yourself
    
        
    Unlock with Chrome Remote Desktop
    
        
    Set up once on the PC: open remotedesktop.google.com/access in Chrome on the PC, choose “Set up remote access” and pick a PIN. On a phone, the Chrome Remote Desktop app opens.
    
        
    For your safety Windows doesn't let any program or website type your password into the lock screen, so Albatross can't do it for you and never asks for it. As soon as the PC is unlocked this continues by itself.
