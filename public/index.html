Turn your PC on and off

Wake-on-LAN: the Albatross server sends a wake-up signal to your home, and your router starts the PC. The PC's details below fill in by themselves while it's connected. One-time setup on the router: see the tip under the fields.
FRITZ!Box: Internet → Permit Access → Port Sharing → your PC → add a sharing for this port, and tick "Start this computer automatically when it is accessed from the internet". In the PC's BIOS, switch on Wake-on-LAN ("Power On By PCI-E"). Saved on this device, and in your account encrypted if you're signed in.
Advanced: smart plug link instead
Used when Wake-on-LAN isn't set up: the button opens this link, for example a Voice Monkey or smart-plug link that switches the PC's plug on. It never goes to the Albatross server.
