How it works

Three steps to take off

Your PC becomes your private AI server. No subscription, no cloud.

Install on your PC

Install Ollama, download a model like qwen3:14b, and start the Albatross host. It runs quietly in the background, even at boot.

Get your code

The control center shows a 16-character code. The first half finds your PC, the second half is your encryption key and never leaves your devices.

Chat from anywhere

Open Albatross on your phone, tablet or laptop, enter the code, and you're talking to your own AI. Install it as an app for full screen.

Jarvis

An assistant that can actually do things

Jarvis lives on your PC as a small orb with eyes. It looks around, blinks, follows your cursor, and gets to work when you ask.

"Hey Jarvis"

Say its name or type, in English or German. It answers out loud with a natural voice, all offline on your PC.

Sees your screen

Ask "what does this error say?" and it reads it to you. It only looks when you ask about the screen.

Uses your PC for you

Opens apps, arranges windows, types and clicks, and asks before anything with consequences. Off until you switch it on.

Watch it, stop it

In Albatross you see your PC's screen live, answer its questions, change its settings, and one big red button stops it at once.

Features

Everything, on your terms

Chat that thinks

Watch reasoning models think live, let them search the web if you want, and give every chat its own instructions.

Images with context

Describe a picture and your PC draws it. Image chats remember earlier requests, so "now make it night" just works.

Files, both ways

Send files from your phone to your PC and back. Share with other people too, only after they accept, encrypted end to end.

Turn your PC on

PC switched off? Save your smart plug link in the settings and Albatross turns it on for you, then it starts sharing by itself.

Smart with your GPU

One model in memory at a time, see what's loaded, and free your graphics card with one click.

Open source

The whole app is public on GitHub. Read exactly what it does, change it, or run your own server.

Privacy

  • Files you send to your PC

FAQ

Questions

Is Albatross free?

Yes, completely. It's open source, and the AI runs on your own hardware, so there's nothing to pay per message.

What kind of PC do I need?

A Windows PC with an NVIDIA graphics card works best. With 8 to 12 GB of graphics memory you can run models up to about 14 billion parameters quickly. Smaller models also run on weaker PCs, just slower.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Open the website on your phone, enter your PC's code, and use it like an app. Add it to your home screen for full screen.

What happens when my PC is off?

Your AI is unavailable until the PC is on. If you save a smart plug link in the settings, Albatross can turn the PC on for you.

Can other people use my AI?

Only people you give your code to. Treat it like a password, and make a new one any time if you shared it by mistake.

Can the server read my chats?

No. Everything between your devices is encrypted with a key only your devices have. The server passes along data it can't read or change.
