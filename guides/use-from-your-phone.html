How to use your own AI from your phone, anywhere

Once the host runs on your PC, every other device can use its AI: your phone on the train, a laptop at a friend's, a tablet on the sofa. The connection is end-to-end encrypted with a code only your devices know.

1. Get your PC's code

Open the Albatross app on the PC and open the Host page or the control center. You'll see a 16-character code such as K7QM-4XPT-9F2C-HW3D. The first half finds your PC; the second half is the encryption key and never leaves your devices. Treat it like a password.

2. Connect your phone

Open albatrossassistent.github.io/public on the phone, go to Settings → Connection, choose Remote and enter the code. The opening screen then shows your phone pinging the PC and the PC pinging Ollama; when both turn green you're connected, and the model list from your PC appears.

3. Install it as an app

For full screen and an icon on the home screen:

  • iPhone and iPad: in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: in Chrome, tap the menu → Install app.

4. What works from the phone

  • Chats with every model on your PC, including pictures made by your image models.
  • Sending files to your PC and back (Beam), encrypted end to end.
  • Watching and stopping Jarvis, the assistant that can use your PC for you.
  • Long answers and pictures keep going on the PC if your phone loses its connection, and arrive when it's back.

When the PC is off

Your AI needs the PC to be running. In Settings → Connection → Turn your PC on and off you can set up Wake-on-LAN (the Albatross server sends a wake-up signal through your router) or, under Advanced, a smart plug link. Albatross then switches the PC on for you, and the host starts sharing by itself once the PC is up. You can also let the PC sleep when nobody uses it.

Letting others use your AI

Only people with your code can connect. For friends and family there's a separate guest code: guests can chat and make pictures, but can't see your files, chats or settings. You can switch guests off at any time on the Host page (Guests can connect).
