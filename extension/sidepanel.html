When “Follow my tab” is on, the AI reads and works in the tab you're looking at. Chrome shows a bar while it's attached; Cancel there pauses it. Buying, sending or deleting always asks you first.

Connect to Albatross

  1. Open Albatross (the button below, or your usual tab).
  2. Settings → Tools → Work on your PC → Browser: My Chrome → Make a code.
  3. The code is copied for you and appears here by itself. If not, paste or type the 6 digits.
